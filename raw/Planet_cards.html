Planet cards are a type of consumable you can obtain throughout a run. They can be obtained through () Celestial Packs, (Creates the {{Planet|}} card for final played [[Poker Hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] of round if {{hl|orange|held}} in hand<br>{{room}} Seal Blue Seal.png) Blue Seals, two specific Tarot cards, and the Shop.

Planet cards can level up their associated poker hand type by increasing the base value of chips and multiplier. Generally, the more difficult a hand is to play, the more chips and multiplier will be added from the Planet card. There is no limit to how many times you can level up your poker hand. Poker hand levels are only kept for the duration of the run, and are reset each time you start a new one.

There are 12 different cards, however three of them - (Increases {{ph|Flush Five}} hand value by {{mult|+3}} and {{chips|+50}} Play {{ph|Flush Five}} during the current run Planet Eris.png) Eris, (Increases {{ph|Flush House}} hand value by {{mult|+4}} and {{chips|+40}} Play {{ph|Flush House}} during the current run Planet Ceres.png) Ceres, and (Increases {{ph|Five of a Kind}} hand value by {{mult|+3}} and {{chips|+35}} Play {{ph|Five of a Kind}} during the current run Planet Planet X.png) Planet X - are secret and not initially available through the methods mentioned above. There are no unlock conditions for Planet cards, with the exception of the three secret planet cards, which can only be found in the shop after playing their associated poker hand type, and only for the rest of that specific run.

Discovering all 12 Planet cards completes the achievement ( Astronomy.jpg) Astronomy and unlocks the (All {{hl|cyan|Planet}} cards and {{hl|cyan|Celestial Packs}} in the shop are {{hl|orange|free}} Discover every {{Planet|}} card Uncommon Effect N/A (Passive) Astronomer.png) Astronomer Joker.

Usage notes

[edit | edit source]
  • Planets, like other Jokers and Consumables, normally can't have duplicates in the shop or Booster Packs without ([[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]], {{Tarot|}}, {{Planet|}}, and {{Spectral|}} cards may appear multiple times Reach Ante level {{hl|orange|4}} Uncommon Effect N/A (Passive) Showman.png) Showman or ([[Booster Packs|{{hl|orange|Celestial Packs}}]] always contain the {{Planet|}} card for your most played [[Poker hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] Base Telescope.png) Telescope. If all 12 Planets are unavailable, subsequent Planet spawns will always be (Increases {{ph|High Card}} hand value by {{mult|+1}} and {{chips|+10}} Available from start. Planet Pluto.png) Pluto.
  • A Royal Flush does not have a Planet card associated with it because it levels up alongside a Straight Flush.

Poker Hands

[edit | edit source]
  • All poker hands directly benefit from their respective Planet card.
    • In general, a higher ranked poker hand will gain more + Chips and + Mult with each level increase.

Card modifiers

[edit | edit source]
  • If a card with a (Creates the {{Planet|}} card for final played [[Poker Hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] of round if {{hl|orange|held}} in hand<br>{{room}} Seal Blue Seal.png) Blue Seal is held at end of round, it creates the Planet card matching the final poker hand played. (Must have room)
    • This is helpful to consider when unlocking planet cards for the first time as it guarantees discovery.
    • (Retrigger all card {{hl|orange|held in hand}} abilities Uncommon Retrigger On Held Mime.png) Mime can retrigger (Creates the {{Planet|}} card for final played [[Poker Hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] of round if {{hl|orange|held}} in hand<br>{{room}} Seal Blue Seal.png) Blue Seal, giving an additional Planet card.

Trivia

[edit | edit source]
  • In the Collection, the first nine planet cards are arranged by their order in the solar system.
  • The top of the artwork on the card highlights the position of the planet in the solar system, except (Increases {{ph|Five of a Kind}} hand value by {{mult|+3}} and {{chips|+35}} Play {{ph|Five of a Kind}} during the current run Planet Planet X.png) Planet X, which has no section highlighted.
  • Similarly, the bottom of the artwork on the card shows the influenced hand type.
  • On mouseover, all planet cards are classed as "Planet", except for (Increases {{ph|High Card}} hand value by {{mult|+1}} and {{chips|+10}} Available from start. Planet Pluto.png) Pluto, (Increases {{ph|Flush House}} hand value by {{mult|+4}} and {{chips|+40}} Play {{ph|Flush House}} during the current run Planet Ceres.png) Ceres, and (Increases {{ph|Flush Five}} hand value by {{mult|+3}} and {{chips|+50}} Play {{ph|Flush Five}} during the current run Planet Eris.png) Eris, which are all classed as "Dwarf Planet"; and (Increases {{ph|Five of a Kind}} hand value by {{mult|+3}} and {{chips|+35}} Play {{ph|Five of a Kind}} during the current run Planet Planet X.png) Planet X, which is classed as "Planet?".
  • Even though (Upgrade every [[Poker Hands|{{hl|purple|poker hand}}]] by {{hl|orange|1}} level Available from start. Spectral Black Hole.png) Black Hole is not a planet card in and of itself, it is still stylized like a traditional planet card.
  • Whenever offered in the shop or created randomly by (Creates up to {{hl|orange|2}} random {{Planet|}} cards<br>{{room}} Available from start. Tarot The High Priestess.png) The High Priestess, planet cards have a cyan spawning animation corresponding to their cyan collection and card colors.
  • In the demo version of Balatro, instead of cyan, planet cards were grey in color but the planets themselves had their normal colors. Some of the planet cards even gave smaller upgrades before getting buffed in future updates before the game's release.
    • Also in the demo, before Booster Packs were added, planet cards were acquired in a unique skip event called Planetarium, where it let you choose 1 of up to 5 random planet cards.
    • The ([[Booster Packs|{{hl|orange|Celestial Packs}}]] always contain the {{Planet|}} card for your most played [[Poker hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] Base Telescope.png) Telescope voucher also had a different effect in the demo, to which it unlocked the Planetarium before every round and let you select 1 of up to 2 random planet cards.
