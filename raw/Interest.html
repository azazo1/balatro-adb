Interest

From Balatro Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

At the end of each round, $1 of interest is earned for every $5 the player has at the end of the round, with a cap of $5 each round (which means that any money over $25 won't generate interest). This incentivizes the player to save money up to a point.

  • (Earn an extra {{Money|1}} of [[interest|{{hl|orange|interest}}]] for every {{Money|5}} you have at end of round Uncommon Economy N/A (Passive) To the Moon.png) To the Moon gives an extra $1 interest for every $5 held at the end of a round.
  • (Raise the cap on interest earned in each round to {{Money|10}} Base Seed Money.png) Seed Money and its upgrade (Raise the cap on interest earned in each round to {{Money|20}} Max out the interest per round earnings for ten consecutive rounds Upgraded Money Tree.png) Money Tree increase the cap on earned interest to $10 and $20 respectively (which means that any money over $50 and $100 won't generate interest respectively). Large amounts of money can be gained per round by combining these vouchers with (Earn an extra {{Money|1}} of [[interest|{{hl|orange|interest}}]] for every {{Money|5}} you have at end of round Uncommon Economy N/A (Passive) To the Moon.png) To the Moon.
