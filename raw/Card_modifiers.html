Card modifiers

From Balatro Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Modifiers are upgrades that can be applied to cards that provide various benefits, such as improved scoring capability, creating consumables, money generation, or card manipulation. There are 4 types of modifiers: Editions, Enhancements, Seals, and Stickers.

Playing cards may each have one Enhancement, one Edition, and one Seal.
Jokers may have one Edition and multiple Stickers.
Consumables (Tarot cards, Planet cards, and Spectral cards) normally cannot have any modifiers, but can be spawned with a Negative edition using (Creates a [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] copy of {{hl|orange|1}} random [[Consumables|{{hl|orange|consumable}}]] card in your possession at the end of the [[The Shop|{{hl|orange|shop}}]] Find this [[Jokers|Joker]] from the [[the Soul|{{hl|sblue|Soul}}]] card. Legendary Effect N/A (Passive) Perkeo.png) Perkeo.
Vouchers and Booster Packs can never have any modifiers.

Whenever a modifier is applied to a card, it retains this modifier for the rest of the run. Applying an enhancement or seal to a card that already has that type (for example, applying a Red Seal to a card that already has a Blue Seal) will replace the previous modifier with the new one. Editions and Stickers are permanent and cannot be changed or overwritten.

In general, modifiers are applied to Jokers and playing cards using Tarot or Spectral cards. Modified playing cards can also be found in Booster Packs, or purchased directly from the Shop by having the ({{hl|orange|Playing cards}} in shop may have an [[Card modifiers#Enhancements|{{hl|sblue|Enhancement}}]], [[Card modifiers#Edition|{{hl|edition|Edition}}]], and/or a [[Card modifiers#Seal|{{hl|orange|Seal}}]] Buy a total of 20 Playing cards from the shop Upgraded Illusion.png) Illusion voucher.

Debuffed cards and Jokers have all modifier effects disabled except for ({{hl|edition|+1}} Joker or Consumable slot Edition Joker (Negative).png) Negative (+1 slot) and ({{chips|+50}}<br>no rank or suit Enhancement Stone Card.png) Stone cards (no rank and suit). Debuffed (Can be used as any suit Enhancement Wild Card.png) Wild cards are considered to be their original suit.

The "Pinned" effect, which cannot be acquired in a normal run, is documented on the ( Eternal, Pinned {{Sticker|Eternal|image=Ceremonial Dagger.png|link=Ceremonial Dagger|name=Ceremonial Dagger|showtooltip=1}}<br>The Pinned Joker is always in the leftmost position) On a Knife's Edge page.

Stickers

[edit | edit source]

Balatro has 11 Stickers that can be applied to Jokers or Decks. They are separated into two categories: in-run Stickers, which have a direct effect on the Joker during the run, and Stake Stickers, which are cosmetic and indicate previous wins with this Joker or Deck.

There are 3 types of in-run Stickers:

AppearanceNameEffect
EternalCan't be sold or destroyed
PerishableDebuffed after 5 rounds
(5 remaining)
RentalLose $3 at end of round

It should be noted that some Jokers cannot be Eternal, while other Jokers cannot be Perishable. All Jokers are compatible with the Rental Sticker.

There are 8 different stake Stickers. As they are fully cosmetic and do not directly impact gameplay during a run, they are not listed here. See the Stake Stickers page for more information about these Stickers.

Appearance rates

[edit | edit source]

When generating cards at random (through the Shop, Booster Packs, etc.), not all modifiers are equally likely. The chance for a modifier to get applied not only depends on the modifier, but also on the type of card, and on purchased Vouchers.

Jokers

[edit | edit source]

Negative Poly Holo Foil Eternal Perishable Rental
None 0.3% 0.3% 1.4% 2% 30% 30% 30%

Glow Up 2.1% 5.6% 8%

Since Jokers can't be both Eternal and Perishable, there is a 40% chance (100% − 30% − 30%) of a Joker being clean of both on Orange and Gold Stakes. There is also a 70% chance (100% − 30%) of not having a Rental sticker on Gold Stake, leading to a combined 28% chance (40% × 70%) of a Joker having no in-run stickers, provided that it has no restrictions on receiving Eternal or Perishable sticker.
