Stakes

From Balatro Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

There are 8 stakes in Balatro, which become cumulatively more difficult. Each new stake modifier is added onto the previous ones, making it more challenging as you progress through the stakes.

In order to unlock the next stake for each unique deck, you must complete a run on the current highest unlocked stake for each specific deck individually. For example, you must complete the White Stake for the ( {{hl|red|+1}} discard every round Unlocked from start Red Deck.png) Red Deck, before you can unlock the Red Stake for the Red Deck. You will not have unlocked the Red Stake for the ( {{hl|blue|+1}} hand every round Discover at least {{hl|orange|20}} items from your [[collection]] Blue Deck.png) Blue Deck, you must first complete the White Stake with the Blue Deck in order to do so.

By finishing a stake with a Joker on the Ante 8 Showdown Blind, you will get a () Stake Sticker on said Joker, with the corresponding Stake color you completed the run on. This also applies to the Deck that was used. By turning on the "display stake sticker during run" in the settings, it will display all stake stickers during your run.

Challenge Decks can only be played on the White Stake.
