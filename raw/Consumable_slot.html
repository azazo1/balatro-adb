Consumable slot

From Balatro Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Consumable slot is a gameplay mechanic in Balatro which, by default, can hold up to 2 consumables at once. Consumables that can be held include:

  • Tarot cards
  • Planet cards
  • Spectral cards

Mechanics

[edit | edit source]

Consumable slots can be used for temporarily holding consumables, such as tarot cards that can modify ranks or suits of playing cards, and then have them used during a round, rather than instantly from Booster Packs.

Consumables can be included in the consumables slots mainly by buying them from the Shop, though they can also be generated from certain Jokers under certain conditions, such as ({{hl|green|1 in 2}} chance to create a {{Tarot|}} card when any [[Booster Packs|{{hl|orange|Booster Pack}}]] is opened <br> {{room}} Common Effect N/A (Passive) Hallucination.png) Hallucination, (Create a {{Tarot|}} card if hand is played with {{$|4}} or less Rare Effect Independent Vagabond.png) Vagabond, (If [[Poker hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] is a {{ph|Straight Flush}}, create a random {{Spectral|}} card <br> {{room}} Uncommon Effect Independent Séance.png) Séance, or (If {{hl|orange|first hand}} of round is a single {{hl|orange|6}}, destroy it and create a {{Spectral|}} card <br> {{room}} Uncommon Effect N/A (Passive) Sixth_Sense.png) Sixth Sense. Some decks and challenges may include consumables in the consumables panel at the start, with some even having an altered maximum number of consumable slots.

If a Tarot card, such as (Creates up to {{hl|orange|2}} random {{hl|purple|Tarot}} cards<br>{{room}} Available from start. Tarot The Emperor.png) The Emperor and (Creates up to {{hl|orange|2}} random {{Planet|}} cards<br>{{room}} Available from start. Tarot The High Priestess.png) The High Priestess, both of which spawn 2 consumables each, has 1 other consumable present, they will generate only 1 consumable instead, particularly if the maximum number of consumable slots is 2.

If the player has an ({{Planet|}} cards in your [[Consumables|{{hl|orange|consumable}}]] area give {{xmult|1.5}} for their specified [[Poker hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] Use a total of {{hl|orange|25}} [[Planet|{{hl|cyan|Planet}}]] cards from any {{hl|cyan|Celestial Pack}} Upgraded Observatory.png) Observatory and some Planet cards on consumable slots, those Planet cards trigger X1.5 Mult after the Jokers get triggered.

Increasing consumable slots

[edit | edit source]

Jokers

[edit | edit source]
  • (Creates a [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] copy of {{hl|orange|1}} random [[Consumables|{{hl|orange|consumable}}]] card in your possession at the end of the [[The Shop|{{hl|orange|shop}}]] Find this [[Jokers|Joker]] from the [[the Soul|{{hl|sblue|Soul}}]] card. Legendary Effect N/A (Passive) Perkeo.png) Perkeo: creates a random ({{hl|edition|+1}} Joker or Consumable slot Edition Joker (Negative).png) Negative copy of a random consumable held in the consumables panel.

Vouchers

[edit | edit source]
  • ({{hl|orange|+1}} consumable slot Base Crystal Ball.png) Crystal Ball: creates an additional consumable slot, increasing the maximum number of consumable slots from 2 to 3.

Editions

[edit | edit source]
  • ({{hl|edition|+1}} Joker or Consumable slot Edition Joker (Negative).png) Negative: adds an extra slot in the consumables panel.
    • Negative consumables can only be introduced from Perkeo.

Decreasing consumable slots

[edit | edit source]

Decks

[edit | edit source]
  • ( Start run with the {{V|Telescope|class=hl-cyan}} voucher <br> {{hl|red|-1}} consumable slot Win a run with the {{D|Blue}} on [[Stakes|any difficulty]] Nebula Deck.png) Nebula Deck: -1 consumable slot, reducing the maximum number of consumable slots from 2 to 1.
