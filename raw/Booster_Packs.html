Booster Packs

From Balatro Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Two new Booster Packs are always offered in each Shop, often but not always of two different types. Booster Packs do not refresh when rerolling the Shop. They come in five categories:

  • Arcana Packs, containing Tarot cards for immediate use.
  • Celestial Packs, containing Planet cards for immediate use.
  • Standard Packs, containing Playing cards (often with Enhancements, Seals and/or Editions) that are added into your deck.
  • Buffoon Packs, containing Joker cards to add to your run.
  • Spectral Packs, containing Spectral cards for immediate use.

Each category of Booster Pack comes in three sizes - Normal, Jumbo and Mega - with each providing more total options than the previous one. The pack will always contain the full amount of cards stated when opened despite the "up to" phrasing, but can in some cases contain duplicate cards. The amount of cards the player can choose depends on the pack size, choosing up to 2 cards from Mega sized packs and up to 1 card from Normal and Jumbo sized packs. Within each size, there is a variety of artwork which can be shown on the Booster Pack; this does not affect the behavior of the Booster Pack.

Purchasing a Booster Pack opens it immediately, taking the player out of the shop, and revealing a set amount of card options for the player to choose from. For Tarot and Spectral Packs, a hand of Playing cards equal to the player's current hand size is dealt for the player to apply effects on while in the pack. The player is able to use any consumables they currently have in their consumable slots while in the pack.

The player is not required to select an option, and can choose to skip at any point if none of the (remaining) cards offered are appealing. Skipping or selecting the final choice from the pack will immediately close the pack and put the player back in the shop. Skipping any booster pack will scale (This Joker gains {{Mult|+3}} when any [[Booster Packs|{{hl|orange|Booster Pack}}]] is skipped <br> {{Currently|{{mult|+0}}}} Common Additive Mult Independent Red_Card.png) Red Card when held, including skipping a Mega sized pack after having already chosen one of the offered cards.

Booster Pack rates

[edit | edit source]

The Booster Packs offered to the player are determined using different probabilities based on their Type and Size. Each kind of Booster Pack has a weight assigned to it, which determines how likely it is to be drawn; for instance, a Jumbo Arcana Pack has four times the likelihood to be offered than a Mega Arcana Pack, but is half as likely to be offered as a Normal Arcana Pack.
