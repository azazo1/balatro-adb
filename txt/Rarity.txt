Jokers are the face of Balatro's gameplay. They hold a significant importance in score generation and provide a multitude of other perks and utilities, such as giving money or creating consumable items like Tarot cards, Planets, and in some cases Spectral cards, or in the specific case of (When [[Blind|{{hl|orange|Blind}}]] is selected, create {{hl|orange|2}} [[Common Jokers|{{hl|blue|Common}} {{hl|orange|Jokers}}]] <br> {{room}} Common Effect On Blind Select Riff-Raff.png)[] Riff-Raff, other Jokers. Jokers are not played alongside standard cards or included in Decks, but rather can be purchased in the Shop or sometimes obtained through other means. They are held in the player's dedicated Joker slots until sold or destroyed.

Every non-challenge run, except those on ( {{hl|orange|+1}} Joker slot <br> {{hl|blue|-1}} hand every round Discover at least {{hl|orange|100}} items from your [[collection]] Black Deck.png)[] Black Deck and ( {{hl|orange|+2}} hand size, <br> {{hl|red|-1}} Joker slot Win a run with any [[Decks|deck]] on the {{Stake|Green}} difficulty or harder Painted Deck.png)[] Painted Deck, will begin with 5 empty Joker slots. Spectral cards, Editions (as explained below), or deck effects like those previously mentioned may allow the player to gain or lose some of these Joker slots. Every Joker is unique and can be used in many ways to create synergy with other Jokers' effects, deck composition, consumables, or all three. Jokers are almost always a requirement to meet the required score of blinds, especially in higher antes, where the required score to defeat each Blind grows exponentially.

As of update 1.0.1o-FULL, there are 150 total Jokers in Balatro. 105 of these are readily accessible upon the player's very first run, but the remaining 45 must be unlocked through the completion of certain conditions, some of which can only be done over the course of multiple runs.

The appearance rates of the four Joker rarities are: Common – 70%. Uncommon – 25%. Rare – 5%. Legendary – only available through (Creates a [[Legendary Jokers|{{hl|purple|Legendary}}]] Joker <br> {{room}} Available from start. Spectral The Soul.png)[] The Soul, which has 0.3% chance of appearing in Spectral/Arcana Packs.

After winning a run by beating the Boss blind of ante 8, the Jokers the player has at that time will receive a Sticker colour of the Stake it was used to win on. The colour in the collection will be of the highest stake the Joker was used. For example, after beating Orange Stake, a (Every played {{hl|orange|card}} permanently gains {{Chips|+5}} when scored Uncommon Chips On Scored Hiker.png)[] Hiker with no sticker will receive the ()[][] Orange Stake sticker, while beating Red Stake with a (Adds the sell value of all other owned [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Jokers}}]] to Mult <br> {{currently|{{mult|+1}}}} Sell a total of {{hl|orange|20}} Joker cards Common Additive Mult Independent Swashbuckler.png)[] Swashbuckler with the ()[][] Purple Stake sticker will not give it a ()[][] Red Stake sticker. Instead, it will preserve the Purple Stake sticker.

Rarities

[edit | edit source]

Jokers have 4 different rarities, denoting how likely they are to appear in a given run: Common (61), Uncommon (64), Rare (20), and Legendary (5). In general, higher rarity jokers are more powerful, but this is not always the case.

When the game randomly generates Jokers, either in the shop or using other joker generation methods, there is a 70% chance of generating a Common Joker, a 25% chance of generating an Uncommon Joker, and a 5% chance of generating a Rare Joker. Legendary Jokers can't appear in the shop, but only through (Creates a [[Legendary Jokers|{{hl|purple|Legendary}}]] Joker <br> {{room}} Available from start. Spectral The Soul.png)[] The Soul Spectral card.

Important Joker Terms

[edit | edit source]

Jokers have a number of key terms that are vital in understanding how they work. These terms are marked with different colors, and by looking at them the player can gain a better understanding of how the joker functions, in case the wording is not clear to them:

- When Scored: The effect triggers when a card is scored. If a card that is played can trigger this Joker, but is not scored, either from not being part of the scored poker hand or from being debuffed by a Boss Blind, the effect will not trigger.
- Contains: The hand has the referenced hand as a part of it. For example, a Three of a Kind "contains" a Pair, despite scoring as a Three of a Kind. A Four of a Kind played with five cards of the same suit "contains" a Flush, and will trigger Flush Joker effects. Refer to the hand information in-game for a more detailed breakdown on which hands contain others. Notably, a Four of a Kind is not considered to "contain" Two Pair, since Two Pair requires two different ranks to be counted, which means that a Four of a Kind will not trigger ({{Mult|+10}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Two Pair}} Common Additive Mult Independent Mad Joker.png)[] Mad Joker or ({{Chips|+80}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Two Pair}} Common Chips Independent Clever Joker.png)[] Clever Joker, and it will not scale (This Joker gains {{Mult|+2}} if played hand contains a [[Poker hands|{{hl|orange|Two Pair}}]] <br> {{currently|{{mult|+0}}}} Uncommon Additive Mult Mixed Spare Trousers.png)[] Spare Trousers. The same can be said for Five of a Kind and Full House, or Flush Five and Flush House.
- Is: In contrast, "poker hand is a pair" means the whole hand is classified as a pair, and the cards played don't constitute a higher-ranked hand.
- When Blind is Selected: The effect triggers when you select a Blind. It will not trigger if the Blind is skipped. In the case of (When {{hl|orange|Small Blind}} or {{hl|orange|Big Blind}} is selected, gain {{xmult|0.5}} and {{hl|orange|destroy}} a random Joker <br> {{Currently|{{xmult|1}}}} Uncommon Multiplicative Mult Independent Madness.png)[] Madness, it only triggers on the ( Standard Small Blind.webp)[] Small Blind and ( Standard Big Blind.webp)[] Big Blind, hence showing "When Small Blind or Big Blind is Selected".
- In Deck: The effect depends on the remaining cards in your deck, not including cards in hand, played, or discarded.
- In Full Deck: This counts some attribute of your complete deck, referencing all cards whether they were drawn, played, discarded, or are held in hand. For the specific case of ({{Mult|+4}} for each card below {{hl|orange|[the deck's starting size]}} in your full deck <br> {{Currently|{{Mult|+0}}}} Uncommon Additive Mult Independent Erosion.png)[] Erosion, this counts how many cards have been removed from the original deck.
- Add: Adds a playing card to your deck permanently.
- Destroy: Remove the target card permanently. If a Joker is destroyed, no money or bonuses are gained that selling it would give.
- Create: Creates a certain type of card, be it a consumable or Joker. This action always requires room for the card in question to take up, with the sole exception of (Creates a [[Negative|{{hl|edition|Negative}}]] copy of {{hl|orange|1}} random [[Consumables|{{hl|orange|consumable}}]] card in your possession at the end of the [[The Shop|{{hl|orange|shop}}]] Find this [[Jokers|Joker]] from the [[the Soul|{{hl|sblue|Soul}}]] card. Legendary Effect N/A (Passive) Perkeo.png)[] Perkeo being able to create ({{hl|edition|+1}} Joker or Consumable slot Edition Joker (Negative).png)[] Negative consumables.
- Gains: Adds a (usually) permanent effect to the target card. This is usually referring to the Joker with the effect, though not always, as seen with (Every played {{hl|orange|card}} permanently gains {{Chips|+5}} when scored Uncommon Chips On Scored Hiker.png)[] Hiker.
- Retrigger: This Joker will score a card again under certain conditions outlined by the Joker. This extra score will activate all effects that take place when the card scores, including effects from other Jokers, enhancements, editions, seals (besides (Retrigger this card {{hl|orange|1}} time Seal Red Seal.png)[][] Red Seal), and chips from the card itself. Note that (Retrigger all card {{hl|orange|held in hand}} abilities Uncommon Retrigger On Held Mime.png)[] Mime only retriggers cards held in hand, and so it will not retrigger editions or chips from the card itself, as the card is not being played and scoring.
- Debuffed: This Joker and its edition can no longer trigger. It retains its full sell value (and can still be used by Swashbuckler/Temperance/etc...). It can still be targeted by random effects such as (When {{hl|orange|Small Blind}} or {{hl|orange|Big Blind}} is selected, gain {{xmult|0.5}} and {{hl|orange|destroy}} a random Joker <br> {{Currently|{{xmult|1}}}} Uncommon Multiplicative Mult Independent Madness.png)[] Madness or ({{hl|green|1 in 4}} chance to add [[Foil|{{hl|edition|Foil}}]], [[Holographic|{{hl|edition|Holographic}}]], or [[Polychrome|{{hl|edition|Polychrome}}]] edition to a random [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]] Available from start. Tarot The Wheel of Fortune.png)[] The Wheel of Fortune, but not by the ( Showdown Crimson Heart.webp)[] Crimson Heart Boss blind. Joker effects that trigger on certain other Jokers, like ([[Uncommon Jokers|{{hl|green|Uncommon}}]] Jokers each give {{xmult|1.5}} Rare Multiplicative Mult On Other Jokers Baseball Card.png)[] Baseball Card does, still trigger even if the other Joker is debuffed. If you win a run with a debuffed Joker, it will still receive the corresponding stake sticker.

Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- With the exception of Legendary Jokers, all Joker art includes the word "Joker" (though it is obscured on ({{suit|Hearts}} and {{suit|Diamonds}} count as the same suit, {{suit|Spades}} and {{suit|Clubs}} count as the same suit Have at least {{hl|orange|3}} [[Wild Cards|{{hl|orange|Wild Card}}]] in your deck Uncommon Effect N/A (Passive) Smeared Joker.png)[] Smeared Joker).
- Originally Balatro was only going to have 120 Jokers. This changed after a miscommunication with Playstack and LocalThunk discussing 150 Jokers, resulting in LocalThunk creating 30 more.^([1])
- Some jokers not present in the public release of Balatro have been revealed later, such as Chaos Theory, the Toker, and Homework Eating Dog.
- Most of the chip jokers have blue lips instead of the more default red lips.
- The average price of all Jokers is $6.14. If legendary Jokers are excluded, this falls to roughly $5.662.
