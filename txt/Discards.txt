Discards are one of the primary actions that a player can perform during each round and are the main tool that the player has to locate certain cards from their deck to play in each round. There are many effects, both positive and negative, that are able to be triggered upon discarding. Some Jokers like (If {{hl|orange|first discard}} of round has only {{hl|orange|1}} card, destroy it and earn {{Money|3}} Uncommon Economy On Discard Trading Card.png)[] Trading Card or (Upgrade the level of the first {{hl|orange|discarded}} poker hand each round Sell 50 cards. Rare Effect On Discard Burnt Joker.png)[] Burnt Joker will activate on the player's first discard but will not reactivate on subsequent discards. Other jokers like ({{Chips|+30}} for each remaining {{hl|orange|discard}} Common Chips Independent Banner.png)[] Banner or (Earn {{Money|2}} per {{hl|orange|discard}} if no discards are used by end of the round Common Economy N/A (Passive) Delayed_Gratification.png)[] Delayed Gratification will have a stronger effect if the player has more unused discards.

By default, each run will start with a total of 3 discards per round (2 discards on ( Blue stake.png)[] Blue Stake or above), however, this number is able to be modified to be higher or lower depending on the deck or challenge deck being used. For example, the ( {{hl|red|+1}} discard every round Unlocked from start Red Deck.png)[] Red Deck will increase the starting number of discards by 1 per round; or in the ( {{hl|red|6}} discards per round<br>{{hl|orange|5}} hand size<br>{{hl|orange|7}} Joker slots {{J|Card Sharp}}<br>{{J|Joker}}Banned Jokers: {{J|Juggler}}, {{J|Troubadour}}, {{J|Turtle Bean}})[] Five-Card Draw challenge deck, the discards per round is set at 6 to offset the starting max hand size of 5.

Usage Notes

[edit | edit source]

There are three main methods for a player to discard cards from their hand:

- Standard Discard: This is when the player selects up to 5 cards they do not want to play so that they can be replaced with up to 5 new cards from the player's deck. Doing so costs 1 discard and will trigger any effects such as (Creates a {{Tarot|}} card when {{hl|orange|discarded}}<br>{{room}} Seal Purple Seal.png)[][] Purple Seal, ({{mult|+1}} per hand played <br> {{mult|-1}} per discard <br> {{Currently|{{mult|+0}}}} Common Additive Mult Mixed Green_Joker.png)[] Green Joker, or (Earn {{Money|5}} for each discarded [[Card Ranks|{{hl|orange|[rank]}}]], rank changes every round Common Economy On Discard Mail-In Rebate.png)[] Mail-In Rebate that activate when discarding a card.
- Throwaway Hand: A common scenario that will occur is that the player will have used all their discards and still not have found the cards they need. In this scenario, the player can elect to play what would be considered to be a throwaway hand where they play as many non-essential cards from their hand as they are able to then draw that many new cards from their deck. In doing so, they are using a hand as if it was a discard. Using throwaway hands comes at a cost though, as each additional hand that the player has upon finishing the round would normally be converted to $1.
- If playing a hand type that requires less than 5 cards, any additional cards that are not scored can be pseudo-discarded, allowing for your discards to be utilized when really needed.

One effective way that pseudo-discards can be used is with (This Joker gains {{Mult|+1}} per {{hl|orange|consecutive}} hand played without a scoring {{hl|orange|face}} card <br> {{Currently|{{Mult|+0}}}} Common Additive Mult Mixed Ride the Bus.png)[] Ride the Bus as the player can use the following methods to remove face cards from their hand using pseudo-discards without resetting the Mult on Ride the Bus:

- The player can play a High Card Hand using 1 Ace and up to 1 King, 1 Queen, and 1 Jack, as long as the hand does not also contain a 10 to form a straight or all match the same suit to form a flush.
- The player can play a Pair, Three of a Kind, or Four of a Kind using any non-face card rank plus up to 1 King, 1 Queen, and 1 Jack.

Using pseudo-discards is also a very effective method when facing ( Boss The Psychic.webp)[] The Psychic as it can be used to allow hands types with less than 5 cards to be valid for scoring.

Poker hands

[edit | edit source]

While all hands can benefit from discarding, hands that are difficult to make, such as Straights, Four of a Kinds, and Flushes benefit the most. Hands like Two Pair, Pair, and especially High Card are generally easy to draw so discards aren't as necessary for these hand types, however, discarding can still be very beneficial when playing these hands if you first want to find specific cards for effects such as (If {{hl|orange|first hand}} of round has only {{hl|orange|1}} card, add a permanent copy to deck and draw it to {{hl|orange|hand}} Rare Effect On Played DNA.png)[] DNA to trigger duplicate if you have modified cards in your deck.

This is even easier and more beneficial if you have a reduced number of discards. However, this can cause issues where you prematurely use all your discards if you are unable to win the round in a single hand.

-  (Earn {{Money|2}} per {{hl|orange|discard}} if no discards are used by end of the round Common Economy N/A (Passive) Delayed_Gratification.png)[] Delayed Gratification only gives money if no discards were used, forcing you to either not use any discards or lose out on a payout at the end of the round.
-  ({{Chips|+30}} for each remaining {{hl|orange|discard}} Common Chips Independent Banner.png)[] Banner is similar to Delayed Gratification discouraging you from using discards. However, it will only decrease in power when discarding, rather than being completely disabled.

-  ({{Chips|+2}} for each remaining card in {{hl|orange|deck}} <br> {{currently|{{chips|+104}}}} Common Chips Independent Blue Joker.png)[] Blue Joker will have its Chip value decreased with each card that is discarded.
-  (Every {{hl|orange|played card}} counts in scoring Common Effect N/A (Passive) Splash.png)[] Splash will prevent you from using pseudo-discards as all cards will score including ones you do not want.
