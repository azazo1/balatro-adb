Tarot cards are a form of consumable you can obtain during a run. They can be acquired via ()[] Arcana Packs, (Creates a {{Tarot|}} card when {{hl|orange|discarded}}<br>{{room}} Seal Purple Seal.png)[][] Purple Seals, the Shop or Jokers like (Create a {{Tarot|}} card when [[Blinds|{{hl|orange|Blind}}]] is selected <br> {{room}} Discover every Tarot card. Uncommon Effect On Blind Select Cartomancer.png)[] Cartomancer.

Tarot cards are used to modify the cards in in your deck, such as changing their suit or rank. They are also capable of giving cards Enhancements, or additional effects, like giving extra chips or multiplier when scored. Some give cash, generate new Jokers, or even create more consumables. There are 22 different Tarot cards available, and they unlock when purchased or used in an unseeded run.

Discovering all the Tarot cards completes the achievement ( Cartomancy.jpg)[] Cartomancy and unlocks (Create a {{Tarot|}} card when [[Blinds|{{hl|orange|Blind}}]] is selected <br> {{room}} Discover every Tarot card. Uncommon Effect On Blind Select Cartomancer.png)[] Cartomancer.

Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- The Tarot cards are based on the Major Arcana, which are the 22 trump cards from a standard 78-card pack of divination cards. They are also often used in tarot reading, a form of cartomancy. Some of the Tarot effects have a loose relation to their meanings in divination, although most do not. The remaining 56 cards, which are currently unused in Balatro, are part of a broader set called the Minor Arcana, otherwise known as the Lesser Arcana.
- The artworks are based on the Rider–Waite-Smith tarot deck.
  - However, it seems to omit and change small elements such as removing the angel in (Enhances {{hl|orange|1}} selected card into a [[Wild Card|{{hl|orange|Wild Card}}]] Available from start. Tarot The Lovers.png)[] The Lovers and making (Enhances {{hl|orange|2}} selected cards to [[Mult Cards|{{hl|orange|Mult Cards}}]] Available from start. Tarot The Empress.png)[] The Empress' scepter into a staff.
  - The cards (Enhances {{hl|orange|1}} selected card into a [[Glass card|{{hl|orange|Glass Card}}]] Available from start. Tarot Justice.png)[] Justice (VIII) and (Increases rank of up to {{hl|orange|2}} selected cards by {{hl|orange|1}} Available from start. Tarot Strength.png)[] Strength (XI) are swapped, as this ordering is more standard for other tarot decks.
- Even though (Creates a [[Legendary Jokers|{{hl|purple|Legendary}}]] Joker <br> {{room}} Available from start. Spectral The Soul.png)[] The Soul is not considered a Tarot card by the game, it is styled like a traditional tarot card.
- Standard tarot cards are involved in the usage of 3 card readings or 5 card readings, which ties into the number of Tarot cards you can select from booster packs depending on their size (3 in regular packs and 5 in Jumbo and Mega packs).
  - In the demo version of Balatro, before Booster Packs were added, Tarot cards were acquired during a skip event called Tarot Reading, where you can choose 1 of up to either 3 or 5 random Tarot cards.
