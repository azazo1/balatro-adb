Interest

From Balatro Wiki

Jump to navigation Jump to search

At the end of each round, $1 of interest is earned for every $5 the player has at the end of the round, with a cap of $5 each round (which means that any money over $25 won't generate interest). This incentivizes the player to save money up to a point.
