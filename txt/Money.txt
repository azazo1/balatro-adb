Money

From Balatro Wiki

Jump to navigation Jump to search

Money, referred to as $ in game, is the primary currency in Balatro. It is a crucial resource for the player during a run.

The money currently held can be seen in the lower left corner of in-game interface, or on the starting panel when continuing a game.

Acquiring Money

[edit | edit source]

Players have several ways to increase their cash reserves throughout a run:

Starting Money

[edit | edit source]

Each of the standard decks provides a different starting advantage, with some offering a head start in the financial department:

- Most decks begin a run with a standard amount of money of $4.
- The ( Start with extra {{Money|10}} Discover at least {{hl|orange|50}} items from your [[collection]] Yellow Deck.png)[] Yellow Deck starts with an extra $10 (for a total of $14).

[edit | edit source]

Spending Money

[edit | edit source]

Players can use money in these ways to meet their needs:

Shop

[edit | edit source]

Money is primarily spent in the Shop, which appears between each round. Here, players can purchase a variety of items, such as Jokers and Consumables, to enhance scoring. The cost of items varies, see Calculating buy cost.

Rerolling

[edit | edit source]

With vouchers (Reroll [[Blinds|Boss Blind]] {{hl|orange|1}} time per Ante, {{Money|10}} per roll Base Director's Cut.png)[] Director's Cut and (Reroll [[Blinds|Boss Blind]] {{hl|orange|unlimited}} times, {{Money|10}} per roll Discover 25 Blinds Upgraded Retcon.png)[] Retcon, players can reroll Boss Blinds for $10.

Achievements & Unlocks

[edit | edit source]

Some items/achievements in the game are unlocked with the use of money.

-  (Earn {{Money|1}} at end of round per unique {{hl|cyan|Planet}} card used this run Have at least {{Money|400}}. Uncommon Economy N/A (Passive) Satellite.png)[] Satellite/ ( Nest Egg.jpg)[] Nest Egg is unlocked by having $400 in one run.

Debt

[edit | edit source]

Under normal circumstances, the minimum amount of money held is $0. However, with Joker (Go up to {{hl|red|-$20}} in debt Common Economy N/A (Passive) Credit Card.png)[] Credit Card, the player can have up to $20 in debt.

Passive effects, including ( Boss The Tooth.webp)[] The Tooth and (Lose {{$|3}} at end of round Sticker Rental Sticker.png)[][] Rental Sticker can also reduce money to below zero.

Effects that doubles the money, like ( Economy tag.png)[] Economy Tag and (Doubles money<br>{{hl|grey|(Max of {{Money|20}})}} Available from start. Tarot The Hermit.png)[] The Hermit, won't double the debt.

Apart from obtaining money to repay debts, (Creates a random [[Rare Jokers|{{hl|red|Rare}} {{hl|orange|Joker}}]], sets money to {{$|0}} Available from start. Spectral Wraith.png)[] Wraith and ( Boss The Ox.webp)[] The Ox can also remove debts by setting money to $0.
