Vouchers

[]

Vouchers are items offered in the shop and refresh or restock in the shop after defeating the boss blind of each ante, or by claiming the ( Voucher tag.png)[] Voucher Tag and skipping a blind. Unlike consumable items, Vouchers and their effects are permanent and remain effective for the entirety of a run. There are 32 Vouchers in total.

The Vouchers are split into 16 pairs, each affecting a different aspect of gameplay. Within each pair is a weaker "initial" Base Voucher and a stronger "dependent" Upgraded Voucher that generally enhances the effect of the former. No Voucher can be claimed twice in a single run normally, and only by claiming a Base Voucher can the player then claim the corresponding Upgraded Voucher in future shops in the run. The Upgraded Vouchers all need to be unlocked by meeting certain conditions and are unavailable at the start of the game. The Base Vouchers, however, are available unconditionally from the beginning.

The chance for each Voucher to appear is the same, and the same Voucher can show up multiple times during a run if not yet purchased. If the shop runs out of Vouchers, due to all unlocked Vouchers having been spawned by the ( Voucher tag.png)[] Voucher Tag or bought, only ({{hl|gray|Does nothing?}} Base Blank.png)[] Blank Vouchers can show up.

Each voucher normally costs $10. The (All cards and packs in shop are {{hl|orange|25%}} off Base Clearance Sale.png)[] Clearance Sale and (All cards and packs in shop are {{hl|orange|50%}} off Redeem at least 10 Voucher cards in one run Upgraded Liquidation.png)[] Liquidation Voucher reduce the price of Vouchers, although it is not explicitly listed in their description.

Discovering every Voucher completes the achievement ( Extreme Couponer.jpg)[] Extreme Couponer.
