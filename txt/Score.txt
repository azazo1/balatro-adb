Score is the determining factor for whether or not the player beats a Blind. The total score the player has during the current Blind is the sum of the score the player achieved for every played hand during the Blind. The score of a single hand is calculated by multiplying the Chips and Mult the player gained for that hand.

Failure to obtain enough score to beat the blind almost always results in an instant game over. Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- When the player plays a hand that exceeds the blind's score requirement, the Chips and Mult squares will show flames.
  - If the player selects a batch of playing cards where the Poker Hand's score exceeds the blind's score requirement, such as selecting five cards of the same rank (Five of a Kind) in a blind with a score requirement of 600, the Chips and Mult will show flames before playing the hand.
