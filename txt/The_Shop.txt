The Shop is the location where Jokers, Consumables, and Vouchers can be acquired by spending money. The Shop is only accessible after defeating a ( Standard Small Blind.webp)[] Small Blind, ( Standard Big Blind.webp)[] Big Blind, or Boss Blind.

By default, the shop sells:

- 2 random cards (usually Jokers, but occasionally Tarot, Planet, and Playing cards if you have purchased the ({{hl|orange|Playing cards}} can be purchased from the [[The Shop|{{hl|orange|shop}}]] Base Magic Trick.png)[] Magic Trick voucher, or Spectral cards if you are playing with ( {{Spectral|}} cards may appear in the shop, start with a {{spectral|Hex}} card Win a run with the {{D|Yellow}} on [[Stakes|any difficulty]] Ghost Deck.png)[] Ghost Deck.)
- 2 random Booster Packs (except for the first visit to any Shop in a run, where one normal Buffoon Pack is guaranteed)
- 1 Voucher

Weights

[edit | edit source]

Weights determine how likely a card is to appear in the given set. The higher the weight, the more it appears. The random cards sold are drawn with the default weights:

- Joker: 20
- Tarot: 4
- Planet: 4

This can be rewritten to the approximate chances of each card appearing as following (note that these are rounded):

- Joker: 71.4%
- Tarot: 14.3%
- Planet: 14.3%

In addition, when a Joker is generated, the different rarities have specific weights as well. The default values for these are:

- Common: 70%
- Uncommon: 25%
- Rare: 5%

Reroll

[edit | edit source]

The player may pay money to reroll the shop an unlimited number of times, removing any of the remaining random cards and replacing them with 2 new random cards. Note that because the rerolls are random, cards seen before rerolling can also appear again. The reroll cost starts at $5 and increases by $1 each reroll, resetting to $5 upon entering a new shop. (Rerolls cost {{Money|2}} less Base Reroll Surplus.png)[] Reroll Surplus and (Rerolls cost an additional {{Money|2}} less Reroll the shop a total of 100 times Upgraded Reroll Glut.png)[] Reroll Glut decrease the initial price to $3 and $1, respectively. ({{hl|orange|1}} free {{hl|green|Reroll}} per shop Common Effect N/A (Passive) Chaos_the_Clown.png)[] Chaos the Clown will make the first reroll free. The ( D6 tag.png)[] D6 Tag starts the rerolls on the next shop to $0.

However, rerolling the shop does not restock Booster Packs or the Voucher. Booster packs only restock upon entering a new shop, and the Voucher restocks after defeating the boss blind.

(This Joker gains {{Mult|+2}} per {{hl|orange|reroll}} in [[The Shop|the shop]] <br> {{Currently|{{mult|+0}}}} Uncommon Additive Mult Independent Flash Card.png)[] Flash Card has a special synergy with rerolling, as it scales when rerolling the shop.

Pricing

[edit | edit source]

In short

[edit | edit source]

All items have a buy cost (purchase price) and a sell value, which are recalculated every time the player interacts with the item. These follow consistent formulas for calculating them, with some exceptions applied due to other in-game items.^([1])

Here follows a simplified formula for calculating an item's buy cost and sell value. For more details, see the Calculating buy cost and Calculating sell value sections.

    buy_cost = (base_cost + edition_cost) * discount_percent
    sell_value = floor(buy_cost / 2)

Discounts

[edit | edit source]

After purchasing the Clearance Sale and Liquidation Vouchers, a 25% or 50% discount is applied to the buy cost of all items in the shop. This also indirectly affects the sell value of all items.

-  (All cards and packs in shop are {{hl|orange|25%}} off Base Clearance Sale.png)[] Clearance Sale (25% off) effectively sets discount_percent = 0.75
-  (All cards and packs in shop are {{hl|orange|50%}} off Redeem at least 10 Voucher cards in one run Upgraded Liquidation.png)[] Liquidation (50% off) effectively sets discount_percent = 0.5

Calculating buy cost

[edit | edit source]

1.  Add up the item's:
    1.  Base cost.
    2.  Edition cost (if it has an Edition).
    3.  Inflation costs (if playing the Inflation challenge).
2.  Multiply this total by the discount_percent from the discount vouchers.
3.  Round half down (for example, 8.5 is rounded to 8).
4.  If the buy price after rounding is $0 or lower, set it to $1.
5.  Apply any remaining special cases (such as Astronomer, the Rental Sticker, or the Coupon Tag).

Calculating sell value

[edit | edit source]

1.  Recalculate the item's total buy cost (applying any vouchers bought in-between, any special case that changes the buy cost, etc.).
2.  Divide the total buy cost by 2.
3.  Floor the result of the division (for example, 3.75 is rounded to 3).
4.  If the sell value after flooring is $0 or lower, set it to $1.
5.  Add any additional sell value gained from Egg or Gift Card.
