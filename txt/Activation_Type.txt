Activation Type

From Balatro Wiki

Jump to navigation Jump to search

Activation is in what order Jokers trigger their effects. Due to the integral Activation Sequence, Joker effects with different activation types can interact very differently.

Joker activation types

[edit | edit source]

On Played

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that activate immediately when you play cards, and before scoring. These are often scaling abilities: for example (This Joker gains {{Chips|+4}} if played hand has exactly {{hl|orange|4}} cards <br> {{Currently|{{Chips|0}}}} Common Chips Mixed Square_Joker.png)[] Square Joker's scaling, (Gains {{Chips|+15}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Straight}} <br> {{Currently|{{chips|+0}}}} Common Chips Mixed Runner.png)[] Runner's scaling, (This Joker gains {{xmult|0.1}} per scoring {{hl|orange|Enhanced card}} played, removes card {{hl|orange|Enhancement}} <br> {{Currently|{{xmult|1}}}} Uncommon Multiplicative Mult Mixed Vampire.png)[] Vampire's scaling, ({{hl|green|1 in 4}} chance to upgrade level of played [[Poker hands|{{hl|orange|poker hand}}]] Uncommon Effect On Played Space Joker.png)[] Space Joker, (If {{hl|orange|first hand}} of round has only {{hl|orange|1}} card, add a permanent copy to deck and draw it to {{hl|orange|hand}} Rare Effect On Played DNA.png)[] DNA, and (All played {{hl|orange|face}} cards become [[Gold cards|{{hl|orange|Gold}}]] cards when scored Uncommon Effect On Played Midas_Mask.png)[] Midas Mask. These effects benefit from playing more required hands or cards. Note that these affect unscored cards.

On Scored

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that activate on every scored card from your played hand: for example (Played cards with {{Suit|Spade}} suit give {{Mult|+3}} when scored Common Additive Mult On Scored Wrathful Joker.png)[] Wrathful Joker, (Played cards with [[Card Ranks|{{hl|orange|odd}}]] rank give {{Chips|+31}} when scored <br> {{hl|gray|(A, 9, 7, 5, 3)}} Common Chips On Scored Odd Todd.png)[] Odd Todd, (Each played [[Card Ranks|{{hl|orange|[rank]}}]] of [[Card Suits|{{hl|orange|[suit]}}]] gives {{xmult|2}} when scored<br><small>Card changes every round</small> In one hand, earn at least {{hl|orange|1,000,000}} chips Uncommon Multiplicative Mult On Scored The Idol.png)[] The Idol, (This Joker gains {{Chips|+8}} when each played {{hl|orange|2}} is scored <br> {{Currently|{{chips|+0}}}} Win a run in {{hl|orange|18}} or fewer rounds Rare Chips Mixed Wee Joker.png)[] Wee Joker's scaling, and (Retrigger all played cards in {{hl|orange|final hand}} of the round Uncommon Retrigger On Scored Dusk.png)[] Dusk. Since they activate on card triggers, they would benefit greatly from card retriggers (except other retriggers), and be hindered by cards not scored or debuffed.

On Held

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that activate on certain cards held in hand: for example (Adds {{hl|orange|double}} the rank of {{hl|orange|lowest}} ranked card held in hand to Mult Common Additive Mult On Held Raised Fist.png)[] Raised Fist, (Each {{hl|orange|King}} held in hand gives {{xmult|1.5}} Rare Multiplicative Mult On Held Baron.png)[] Baron, (Each {{hl|orange|face}} card held in hand has a {{hl|green|1 in 2}} chance to give {{Money|1}} Common Economy On Held Reserved Parking.png)[] Reserved Parking, and (Retrigger all card {{hl|orange|held in hand}} abilities Uncommon Retrigger On Held Mime.png)[] Mime. They benefit from retriggers from Mime (except for itself) and are affected by debuffs.

Independent

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that happen after all the cards in hand are scored: for example ({{Mult|+4}} Common Additive Mult Independent Joker.png)[] Joker, ({{Chips|+50}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Pair}} Common Chips Independent Sly Joker.png)[] Sly Joker, (Gives {{xmult|0.2}} for each [[Steel cards|{{hl|orange|Steel Card}}]] in your {{hl|orange|full deck}} <br> {{currently|{{xmult|1}}}} Uncommon Multiplicative Mult Independent Steel Joker.png)[] Steel Joker. Editions on Jokers like foil, holographic or polychrome also activate during this phase. These Jokers do not get affected by retriggers or card debuffs in most cases.

On Other Jokers

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that activate on certain other Jokers. Only ([[Uncommon Jokers|{{hl|green|Uncommon}}]] Jokers each give {{xmult|1.5}} Rare Multiplicative Mult On Other Jokers Baseball Card.png)[] Baseball Card activates this way as of now.

On Discard

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that activate on discarding certain cards or poker hands. There are a few categories: (Upgrade the level of the first {{hl|orange|discarded}} poker hand each round Sell 50 cards. Rare Effect On Discard Burnt Joker.png)[] Burnt Joker and (Earn {{Money|5}} if {{hl|orange|3}} or more {{hl|orange|face cards}} are discarded at the same time Common Economy On Discard Faceless_Joker.png)[] Faceless Joker rely on discarding poker hands; (This Joker gains {{Chips|+3}} per discarded [[Card Suits|{{hl|orange|[suit]}}]] card, suit changes every round <br> {{currently|{{chips|+0}}}} Uncommon Chips Mixed Castle.png)[] Castle's scaling and (Earn {{Money|5}} for each discarded [[Card Ranks|{{hl|orange|[rank]}}]], rank changes every round Common Economy On Discard Mail-In Rebate.png)[] Mail-In Rebate rely on discarding specific cards. Benefits from having more discards, but doesn't have much synergy besides that.

Mixed

[edit | edit source]

Jokers that belong to multiple other categories, such as (Gains {{Chips|+15}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Straight}} <br> {{Currently|{{chips|+0}}}} Common Chips Mixed Runner.png)[] Runner belonging to both "On Played" for scaling, and "Independent" for adding chips to the total score.

On Blind Select

[edit | edit source]

Joker effects that activate when a Blind is selected, such as (When [[Blind|{{hl|orange|Blind}}]] is selected, create {{hl|orange|2}} [[Common Jokers|{{hl|blue|Common}} {{hl|orange|Jokers}}]] <br> {{room}} Common Effect On Blind Select Riff-Raff.png)[] Riff-Raff and (Create a {{Tarot|}} card when [[Blinds|{{hl|orange|Blind}}]] is selected <br> {{room}} Discover every Tarot card. Uncommon Effect On Blind Select Cartomancer.png)[] Cartomancer. These effects trigger from the specific game action of selecting a Blind, do not trigger if the Blind is skipped, and can be copied by (Copies ability of [[Jokers|{{Hl|orange|Joker}}]] to the right Win a run Rare Effect Blueprint.png)[] Blueprint and (Copies the ability of leftmost [[Jokers|{{hl|orange|Joker}}]] Discard a {{ph|Royal Flush}}. Rare Effect Brainstorm.png)[] Brainstorm.

N/A (Passive)

[edit | edit source]

Some non-scoring Jokers fit in none of the above categories, mostly Economy Jokers like (Earn {{Money|4}} at end of round Common Economy N/A (Passive) Golden_Joker.png)[] Golden Joker, or Effect Jokers like (All {{ph|Flushes}} and {{ph|Straights}} can be made with 4 cards Uncommon Effect N/A (Passive) Four Fingers.png)[] Four Fingers.

Passively active Jokers, since they never specifically activate, cannot be copied by Blueprint or Brainstorm.

Strategy

[edit | edit source]

Since on-scored Jokers and on-held Jokers always activate before independent Jokers, the xMult from on-scored Jokers and +Mult from independent Jokers don't activate in the optimal order. For example, ({{Mult|+10}} if played hand contains a {{ph|Flush}} Common Additive Mult Independent Droll Joker.png)[] Droll Joker cannot benefit from (Each played card with [[Card Suits|{{hl|orange|[suit]}}]] gives {{xmult|1.5}} when scored, <br> <small>suit changes at end of round</small> Rare Multiplicative Mult On Scored Ancient Joker.png)[] Ancient Joker when a flush with the selected suit is played.
