Joker slot is a core gameplay mechanic in Balatro that determines the maximum number of Joker cards a player can hold simultaneously. For most Decks, the default count of Joker slots is 5.

Each Joker the player has occupies one of the slots. If all Joker slots are full, the player generally cannot acquire new Jokers without selling or destroying an existing one to free up space. The exception to this rule is the ({{hl|edition|+1}} Joker or Consumable slot Edition Joker (Negative).png)[] Negative edition.
